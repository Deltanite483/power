Fly into terrain to carve craters and trenches. Roads, concrete buildings and trees are world voxels: punch or fly through them to carve holes.

Punch nearby clouds (within 8 m) to blast local holes; fast flight cuts wider tunnels. Openings drift with the cloud and slowly close.

Find your way

Faora-Ul flies with you. Use the pause-menu Friend / Foe roster to set behavior; B toggles her Foe assignment. Press G to call all deployed friends to you; find her cyan marker on the map.

Light unguarded hits only stagger: a shove and a flinch. Only solid hits send anyone flying. Bedrock 2 m below the ground can't be broken.

M Expand map · Esc Release mouse

B Toggle Faora enemy mode · N Summon / dismiss Anissa

R Tap to lock onto the nearest enemy or unlock; hold to home in and land a double-fist ram · block cuts ram damage

Hits reduce health and orientation. The visual disturbance fades as you stabilize; wait for the crossed-out flight icon to clear before flying again. Health regenerates continuously while you are alive, even in combat. With Persistence Mode off, zero HP leaves you downed: a teammate can revive you to 40 HP, up to three times. Mash left click to self-revive, or hold T near a downed friend to help. The fourth defeat is final; Retry / Resummon starts a new life. Enable Persistence Mode in pause for automatic returns after 25 seconds at 75% HP. Everyone gets six seconds of protection after revival.
